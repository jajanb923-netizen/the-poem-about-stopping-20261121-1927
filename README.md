A short poem about agency — the power to stop and to stay.

A river learns a dam not by losing its water but by holding it:
stopping is not surrender, and staying is the harder act — the hand
that does not reach for the switch, the mouth that does not finish
the sentence, the choice made twice to remain the one who chooses.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the poem in
`entry.txt` to the output directory (`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, else
`/output/`), byte-identical, after checking the poem is non-empty,
not whitespace-only, and at most 280 characters.
